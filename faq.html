What does each service tier cost?

Intelligence Audit — $4,840 starting
Applied maps your existing intelligence practices: what you're collecting, where it lives, how it reaches decision-makers. You get a structured gap analysis with prioritized recommendations. Low commitment, high clarity. This is the first paid engagement and the natural foundation for everything that follows. Deliverable: findings brief + recommendations report.

Program Intelligence Brief — $3,227 starting
A structured OSINT deep-dive on one platform, program, contract vehicle, or competitor organization. Sourced from open regulatory filings, aviation registries, government procurement databases, and Applied's curated aerospace intelligence network. Scope and deliverable are fixed before work starts. Deliverable: structured intelligence brief, sourced.

Collection Architecture Review — $6,454 starting
For organizations ready to build or formalize a permanent intelligence capability. Applied assesses your sources, tools, collection workflows, and dissemination pipeline, then delivers a practical blueprint for a sustainable, in-house OSINT function built to your operational tempo and resource constraints. Deliverable: assessment report + architecture blueprint.

Collection Vault Audit — $2,581 starting
An audit of how you personally track sources, entities, and cases right now (Obsidian, notes, whatever it is), plus a redesign spec for making it hold up. Built for OSINT/intelligence analysts and small teams. The lightest entry point into a working collection architecture. Deliverable: written audit + vault redesign spec.

Pricing scales with organizational size and engagement complexity. The rates above are calibrated for small and mid-size operators. Enterprise and prime contractor engagements are scoped individually.

The $300 scoping consultation isn't a separate cost. It credits in full toward whichever tier the engagement becomes.

Who is the Collection Vault Audit for?

Individual analysts and small teams, not organizations. If you're the one person (or the small group) actually doing the collecting, tracking sources and entities in Obsidian, notes, spreadsheets, or whatever system you've cobbled together, this tier is built for you.

It's the lightest entry point Applied offers: a written audit of your current setup plus a redesign spec, not an organization-wide architecture engagement. If you're scoping a capability for a whole team or department, look at the Collection Architecture Review instead.

How is Applied different from other OSINT consultants?

Scope, deliverables, timeline, and price are agreed in writing before work starts, so there's no scope creep and no open-ended hours.

Applied doesn't sell generalist market research. The practice is built on aerospace, defense, and government domain work: open regulatory filings, aviation registries, government procurement databases, and a curated aerospace intelligence network most generalist OSINT shops don't have.

Every tier has a fixed deliverable and a fixed starting price, published up front. You're not requesting a quote into a black box. You know what you're buying before you talk to anyone.

The engagement ladder runs from a $300 scoping consultation through four fixed-scope tiers, from a single analyst's personal vault to a full in-house intelligence architecture. You start at the size that matches your actual problem, not a generic retainer.

Applied formalizes intelligence practices you likely already run informally: tracking competitors, monitoring program announcements, watching regulatory shifts, reading contract awards. The work is building the doctrine, systems, and process to make that deliberate, not starting from zero.

What does an engagement timeline look like, start to finish?
  1. Consultation ($300). A scoping session to confirm fit and identify the right tier. Applied responds to consultation requests within two business days.
  2. Scoping summary. Applied reviews your requirements against available sources and methodologies, then sends a written summary: recommended tier, deliverable, timeline, and price. Nothing starts until you agree to these in writing. The $300 you already paid credits against this price.
  3. Engagement. Applied executes the scoped work: audit, brief, architecture review, or vault audit, depending on the tier selected.
  4. Delivery. You receive the finished deliverable in a structured, decision-ready format, tailored to your environment and reporting requirements.
