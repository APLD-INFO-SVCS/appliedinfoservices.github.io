About Applied

Applied Information Services is an investigative-research firm that answers hard questions from primary sources and public records for business owners, analysts, and defense and aerospace teams.

What Applied does

Investigative Research

$300 for a sourced written memo, delivered in five business days. You send a question, we research it from filings, registrations, court documents, and datasets, and you get the findings with a confidence rating on each one. See how the memo works.

Intelligence Audit

From $4,840. We map the intelligence practices you already run and deliver a gap analysis with prioritized recommendations.

Program Intelligence Brief

From $3,227. A sourced deep-dive on one platform, program, contract vehicle, or competitor organization.

Collection Architecture

From $6,454. An assessment and blueprint for building a permanent in-house open-source research function, scoped from one team to a full program.

What makes Applied different

Prices are published

Every offer has a stated price and a stated deliverable. Many consultancies quote only on request. You know what you are buying before you talk to anyone.

Scope is fixed in writing

Scope, deliverable, timeline, and price are agreed before work starts. Open-ended hourly OSINT contracting leaves the bill open. Applied closes it.

Answers come from primary sources

Applied works from the original records and notes where they disagree. An AI chatbot answer summarizes what is already written. These are the questions where that answer goes wrong.

Confidence is stated and gaps are named

Every finding carries a confidence rating. Open questions are listed on the page, and sources are listed at the end so you can check the work.

The press record, at its real size

The Washington Post credited JANN by name for document analysis in an investigation of a covert CIA program. That is one citation, and it is the kind of work Applied sells.

What is Applied Information Services?

How long does it take?

A research memo takes five business days. Applied replies to every inquiry within two business days.

No. Applied works from open sources and public records only, and has no government or intelligence-community affiliation.
