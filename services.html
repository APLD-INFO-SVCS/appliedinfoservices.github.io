Capabilities

Applied Information Services is a consulting and services firm. Its capabilities span eight areas.

Aerospace Domain Awareness

  • Aircraft Research and Documentation. Registry, ownership, and flight-history research on individual aircraft and fleets.
  • Platform and Fleet Analysis. Documentation of aircraft types, operators, and the roles they fill.
  • Aviation and Defense Market Research. Programs, operators, contract activity, and market structure across the aerospace and defense sector.
  • Program and Acquisition Research. Open-source research on defense and government programs.

Investigative Research and Due Diligence

  • Public Records Research. Filings, registrations, court documents, and datasets, read at the source.
  • Corporate and Ownership Research. Entity structures, beneficial ownership, and the relationships between them.
  • Third-Party Due Diligence. Background research on organizations ahead of a decision.

Open-Source Intelligence

  • Collection and Monitoring. Structured open-source collection on defined subjects over time.
  • Briefings and Analytic Products. Findings presented to decision-makers in person or as written assessments, briefs, and reports in standard formats.

Data, Analytics, and Dissemination

  • Data Processing and Analytics. Collection, cleaning, and analysis of structured and unstructured data.
  • Dissemination. Delivery of finished analysis formatted to the customer's needs.

Automation, AI, and Machine Learning

  • Workflow Automation. Repetitive work moved to automated systems.
  • Applied Machine Learning. Models and AI tools applied to defined problems.
  • AI Adoption Support. Practical integration of AI tools into existing work.

Systems and Knowledge Management

  • Systems and Workflow Design. Information systems built around how the work is done.
  • Knowledge Management. Structures that keep an organization's research findable and usable.
  • Technical Writing and Editing. Documentation, procedures, and reports.

Training and Analyst Development

  • Open-Source Intelligence Training. Instruction in open-source methods for analysts and teams.
