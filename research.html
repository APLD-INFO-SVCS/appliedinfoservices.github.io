What this is

You bring one hard question you cannot answer on your own. We research it from primary sources and public records, check what we find, and hand you a written answer you can act on. Primary sources are the original records: filings, registrations, court documents, datasets. We work from those, note where they disagree, and show the work behind every finding. These are the questions a chatbot answer gets wrong.

What you get

A written memo, two to four pages, built to Applied's deliverable standard. It leads with the answer, then the findings behind it. Every finding carries a confidence rating, so you can see where the evidence is solid and where it is thin. Gaps are named on the page. Sources are listed at the end so you can check the work yourself. One round of clarification questions after delivery is included at no charge.
